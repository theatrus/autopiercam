AutoPierCam 0.2.3 improves Chatstronomy setup and fixes misleading capture and
settings status in the Viewer.

- Pairing is the first step in Chatstronomy setup, with save/status controls
  always visible. Successful or rejected pairing keeps selected sources and
  limits; sharing remains off until explicitly enabled and saved.
- Status refresh and Stop sharing preserve pending sharing edits. Concurrent
  changes require review, and forgetting pairing requires confirmation.
- Startup settling now checks a stable exposure/gain window instead of requiring
  an unchanging scene or unclipped pixels. Moving clouds and bright regions no
  longer hold a steady exposure in startup. Long-exposure deadlines, cancellation,
  and the adaptive controller's adjustment checks remain in place.
- The Viewer distinguishes acquiring preview frames from recording, and refreshes
  the header, counters, and pause controls every two seconds without reloading
  configuration. Settling text explains received frames instead of displaying
  confusing counts such as 60/6.
- Unsaved-change detection compares actual settings with their saved values.
  Loading/formatting controls and rediscovering cameras do not count as edits;
  reverting an edit clears the warning.

Install the signed **AutoPierCam-0.2.3-x64.msi** to update the agent and Viewer.
Existing configuration, captures, and pairing credentials are retained. Upgrading
does not enable image sharing. The MSI and application payloads are signed as
**StackFoundry LLC**. ZWO camera drivers and optional FFmpeg remain separate.

The signed NINA plugin **0.2.3.0** includes the clearer exposure-progress text and
is published through the shared registry at `https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/`
and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`. It requires NINA **3.2.0.9001+** and the
separately installed AutoPierCam agent.
