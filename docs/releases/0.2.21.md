AutoPierCam 0.2.21 adopts Regain 0.5.8 continuous acquisition. SDK remains the
default; existing camera selection, settings and opt-in USB recovery are retained.

## Changes

- Keep the isolated camera owner draining while preview, encoding and storage
  consumers wait. A bounded latest-frame slot prevents an accumulating queue.
  Fractional FPS caps delivery, not the sensor's acquisition rate.
- Use SDK native video instead of repeated SDK single exposures. Direct
  ASI662MC/ASI676MC use native video through 30 seconds and repeated stills above
  that limit. Other Direct families retain their supported still protocols.
- Apply exposure/gain changes at a completed-frame boundary without restarting
  native video. Discard transition frames and account for the previous exposure
  and settling fence in the watchdog. Geometry changes still restart safely.
- Adopt the ASICap-observed Direct long-exposure timing-write order. Normal SDK
  read timeouts back off without restarting; terminal failures remain visible.
- Add continuous-stream counters and settings-generation breadcrumbs to bounded
  fault diagnostics. No extra camera discovery, silent camera selection or
  implicit USB resets are added.
- Preserve 30-second session retries, malformed-frame recovery and optional
  serial-bound USB recovery with administrator rights and a five-minute cooldown.

## Validation and limits

Local operator-authorized full-frame transition checks passed 234 ms through
6.4, 25 and 60 seconds and back on ASI662MC USB 2 and ASI676MC USB 3 through
SDK and Direct. AutoPierCam's SDK adapter also passed the 60-second-to-234-ms
transition on both cameras with the previous-exposure timeout allowance.
Automated tests cover the capture pipeline, pacing, WB, legacy recovery and
isolated worker failures without opening physical cameras.

The remote hard lock was not reproduced; firmware, interference and power remain
unproven explanations. These bounded checks do not guarantee long-running
stability, optical accuracy, cold startup or physical-disconnect recovery.
No private images, camera identities or raw traces are bundled.

## Install or upgrade

Use **AutoPierCam-0.2.21-x64.msi**. Settings and images are preserved; the ZWO
USB driver is still required. Previously published assets remain unchanged.
NINA plugin **0.2.21.0** is published to both
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
Minimum NINA version remains **3.2.0.9001**; the plugin never owns the camera.
