AutoPierCam 0.2.18 adopts Regain 0.5.5 and its experimental ASI662MC Direct USB
video mode. SDK remains the default; existing configurations do not change driver
or enable USB recovery.

## Changes

- Use continuous video for ASI662MC Direct USB exposures through 30 seconds on
  USB 2 and USB 3. Reuse sensor initialization and factory calibration between frames.
- Retain still capture for longer exposures and ASI676MC. The existing exposure
  ceiling is unchanged; there is no silent switch to the SDK.
- Honor fractional FPS limits on video grabs, including settling and adaptive
  exposure changes. Account for intentional worker pacing in stall deadlines.
- Stop video between frames on shutdown, and cancel pending FPS/exposure waits.
- Preserve manual/AWB controls, cached camera identity, discovery backoff,
  30-second automatic retry and optional serial-bound USB port recovery.

## Optional USB recovery

The existing Viewer option to reset the selected camera's port after failure
remains off by default. It requires an explicit serial and an administrator-started
agent, checks identity, and has a five-minute cooldown. No hub-wide reset,
automatic elevation, local installation or installed-settings change is introduced.

## Validation and limits

Regain's operator-authorized Windows ASI662MC matrices passed on USB 2 and USB 3:
21 video frames per transport, full frame/ROI, exposure changes through 30 seconds,
fractional FPS, cancellation, restart and return to still capture. AutoPierCam
adapter and pipeline regressions use simulations, not physical cameras.

FPS limits grabs, not sensor output; slow consumers may receive buffered frames.
Long-running stability, optical accuracy, cold startup, physical USB-disconnect
recovery, Linux/macOS video hardware and the installed Viewer's full driver
handoff remain unvalidated. No private images or camera identities are bundled.

## Install or upgrade

Use **AutoPierCam-0.2.18-x64.msi**. Existing settings and images are preserved.
The ZWO USB driver remains required. Published release assets are unchanged.

NINA plugin **0.2.18.0** must be published to both
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
Minimum NINA version remains **3.2.0.9001**. The plugin never owns the camera.
