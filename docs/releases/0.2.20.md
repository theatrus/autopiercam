AutoPierCam 0.2.20 adopts Regain 0.5.7 and adds bounded camera recovery and
fault diagnostics. SDK remains the default; existing driver and USB recovery
settings are unchanged.

## Changes

- ASI662MC/ASI676MC Direct USB video discards one malformed frame, restarts
  the stream on the same handle and captures a fresh validated frame. FPS and
  cancellation remain respected. Repeated failures remain bounded and visible.
- Retry SDK ASI_EXP_FAILED once on the existing handle without extending the
  outer frame deadline. Arbitrary SDK errors are not silently retried.
- Log fault snapshots with exposure/gain/ROI, elapsed time, last valid frame,
  retry attempts and a bounded history of worker phases. Preserve native errors.
- Keep routine phases at DEBUG, with INFO health summaries at most once per
  minute. Record USB transport at startup without extra camera discovery.
- Keep 30-second session retries and optional serial-bound USB port recovery.
  USB reset remains off by default, requires an explicitly selected serial and
  an elevated agent, and observes a five-minute cooldown. No hub-wide resets.

## Validation and limits

Local capped ASI662MC USB 2 tests passed seven fixed 60-second frames per
backend. Both old and candidate Direct workers passed the gain-300
6.4/25/25/6.4/25-second sequence. The updated AutoPierCam adaptive pipeline
passed 6.4-to-25.6-to-60 seconds, settled after four valid 60-second samples,
saved a still and exited cleanly. Automated recovery tests use simulators only.

The reported remote USB 2 framing failure was not reproduced locally. These
tests establish recovery behavior, not an interference diagnosis or a guarantee
of long-running stability. Optical accuracy, cold startup, physical-disconnect
recovery and Linux/macOS video hardware remain unvalidated. No private images
or camera identities are bundled.

## Install or upgrade

Use **AutoPierCam-0.2.20-x64.msi**. Settings and images are preserved; the ZWO
USB driver is still required. Previously published release assets are unchanged.
NINA plugin **0.2.20.0** is published to both
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
Minimum NINA version remains **3.2.0.9001**; the plugin never owns the camera.
