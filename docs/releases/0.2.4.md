AutoPierCam 0.2.4 adds full-HD previews, a quieter Viewer, and non-disruptive
settings saves.

- Settings are hidden until needed. Use **Settings** to show or hide the panel;
  hiding it preserves edits. Ambiguous camera selection opens the picker.
- Preview overlays show only dimensions and frame age. **Details** contains
  exposure timing, frame counters and diagnostic information. Normal startup
  and loaded settings no longer display warning indicators.
- Preview resolution increases from a 1280-pixel to a 1920-pixel long edge:
  1920x1080 for a full-HD 16:9 camera. Smaller images are not upscaled and saved
  stills retain their configured capture resolution.
- Saving exposure/gain limits, still interval or JPEG quality updates the running
  camera loop without restarting or repeating startup settling. Upload, video
  and retention changes reload recording services without reopening the camera;
  draining pending work may briefly delay frame delivery. Pause state, pending
  capture requests and adaptive day/night history are retained.
- Unchanged saves do not rewrite configuration or reconfigure hardware. Camera
  selection, image dimensions/binning and RAW-format changes still require a
  restart. Saves during initial settling apply after that initial pass.

Install the signed **AutoPierCam-0.2.4-x64.msi** to update the agent and Viewer.
Existing configuration, captures and pairing credentials are retained. Upgrading
does not enable image sharing. The MSI and application payloads are signed as
**StackFoundry LLC**. ZWO camera drivers and optional FFmpeg remain separate.

Update the NINA plugin to **0.2.4.0** together with the agent: older plugins reject
full-HD previews. The signed plugin is published in the shared registry at
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
It requires NINA **3.2.0.9001+** and the separately installed AutoPierCam agent.
Chatstronomy accepts the HD preview source while retaining its bounded network
image size.
