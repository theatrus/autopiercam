AutoPierCam 0.2.28 adds configurable timelapse recording windows and an independent
local-clock send time. Record sunset to sunrise and send the completed movie at
noon in your observing timezone. Existing UTC-day settings keep their behavior.

## Changes

- Choose UTC day, local noon-to-noon, or sunset-to-sunrise recording in Imaging
  settings. Night mode defaults to sunset/sunrise, with optional civil or
  astronomical twilight. Enter observing coordinates and an IANA timezone;
  daylight-saving changes do not turn local noon into a fixed 24-hour countdown.
- Choose immediate Chatstronomy delivery or a local clock time, defaulting to
  noon when selecting Night. Movie sharing remains a separate opt-in. The movie
  must finish encoding first; AutoPierCam does not wake a sleeping PC or start a
  stopped camera to send it. Generic HTTP PUT uploads remain immediate.
- Persist new recording windows and delayed delivery intents across restart.
  Catch-up expires 24 hours after window close to respect the existing Hub
  contract. A changed schedule, location, pairing or sharing revision cancels the
  old intent. An interrupted claimed send is not replayed, avoiding duplicate
  posts at the cost of possibly missing that send.
- Protect a waiting movie from retention while counting it against storage
  limits. Original still retention, immutable upload snapshots, bounded HTTP
  retries, Hub size limits and the 512 MiB client ceiling remain unchanged.
  Discord delivery is best-effort; no MP4 is split, resized or overwritten.
- Include 25-hour local windows in the bounded sample cache. Polar periods with
  no complete sunset/sunrise pair produce no night clip. Solar times are
  minute-resolution estimates, not terrain/weather-aware measurements.

Regain remains pinned to released 0.5.15.1 source (Rust crates 0.5.15). Camera
acquisition, motion images, estimated post-slew freshness and opt-in overlays
are unchanged. Changing the schedule does not abort or restart an exposure.
See [video setup](../video.md) for configuration and failure behavior.

## Validation scope

Software tests cover DST gaps/folds, 23/25-hour windows, midnight-spanning nights,
exact send and expiry boundaries, restart/rollback, consent changes during
snapshot copying, retention protection, native encoding and immutable uploads.
Negative controls remove the send-time guard and replace local-calendar windows
with 24-hour arithmetic; both must fail their regression tests. No physical
camera, optical, long-soak, live Discord upload or local installation is tested.

## Install or upgrade

Use **AutoPierCam-0.2.28-x64.msi** to update the agent and Viewer together. Existing
settings and images are preserved. Configure the new schedule explicitly;
upgrading does not enable movie sharing or change your machine's timezone.
NINA plugin **0.2.28.0** is distributed through both public feeds,
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
Minimum NINA remains **3.2.0.9001**. The plugin never opens a camera handle.
