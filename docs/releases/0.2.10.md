AutoPierCam 0.2.10 makes the Viewer smaller and clearer.

- Compact top bar with the AutoPierCam logo and less padding.
- Preview Details shows live diagnostics, without the resolution paragraph.
- Shorter settings help, sharing instructions, status messages, and upload feedback.

Capture, gain control, and sharing behavior are unchanged.

Install **AutoPierCam-0.2.10-x64.msi** to update the agent and Viewer.
Settings, captures, and pairing credentials are retained. Sharing stays under
your existing settings. The installer and application payloads are signed by
**StackFoundry LLC**. ZWO drivers and optional FFmpeg are installed separately.

NINA plugin **0.2.10.0** is available through both shared registries:
`https://nina-plugins.psf-guard.com/` and `https://nina-plugins.pulsarfab.com/`.
It requires NINA **3.2.0.9001+** and the AutoPierCam agent on the same PC and
Windows account. Plugin behavior is unchanged.
